## 👋 About Me

<p align="center">
  <strong>Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Information Engineering · Undergraduate Student</strong>
</p>

> **Exploring the possibilities of AI and turning ideas into reality through engineering.**

Hi, I'm **Léo WEE**.

I'm interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence and software engineering, especially AI agents, generative AI, developer tools, and applied machine learning.

I enjoy turning ideas into usable products — from requirements analysis and prototyping to implementation, testing, documentation, and continuous improvement. I also care about user experience, verifiable results, and clearly defined product boundaries.

**Areas of interest**

- 🤖 AI agents, agent skills, and tool use
- 🛠️ AI-assisted development and human–AI workflows
- 🧠 Applied machine learning, deep learning, and mathematical modeling
- 🌐 AI-powered web applications, browser extensions, and developer tools
- 🎮 AI-driven or AI-assisted game creation and interactive experiences
- 🥽 Unity, virtual reality, and immersive interaction
- 🗺️ Interactive maps, vectorization, and data visualization

I'm also involved in research projects related to **Unity, virtual reality, and generative AI**.

Recently, I've been focusing more on **portable Agent Skills**, tool reliability, and open-source contributions, with an emphasis on verifiable fixes and clear documentation.
